## 2. Baseline requirements and process decisions

The May 20 approved platform is six modular boards in a star around the Core: ESP32-P4 application MCU, C6 networking co-MCU via esp_hosted SDIO, NFC/WS2812, beeper, PoE, separate 5–32 V DC-in, and 4-inch MIPI-DSI/GT911 carrier. PoE must contain **all Ethernet and power logic**, sending only converted +5 V and RMII/management/reset signals to Core. Core diode-OR combines PoE and DC; DC-only is WiFi-only. Case CAD is explicitly excluded, although board/mechanical envelopes remain required. **P4 is fixed: the proposed S3 fallback was expressly rejected** on May 20 at 19:53 UTC. An off-the-shelf Adafruit-like display is the sourcing fallback. [S1, S2]

ATT-343 is research/preparation; ATT-345 is the sibling implementation parent. Firmware move ATT-344 precedes bootstrap ATT-346; shared contract ATT-347 precedes beeper pipeline proof ATT-348; Phase 2 boards precede ATT-354 integration. User selected phased gates **plus an early physical pipeline proof**. Research sub-issues are created lazily under ATT-343. On May 22, after initial confusion about parenting, user said “stick to spec”; ATT-377 stayed under ATT-343. ATT-376 NFC antenna research was initially called obsolete, then correctly retained because v0 renders/export did not establish functional antenna/card-read acceptance. [S1–S4]

The initial approved documents were gitignored under `docs/superpowers/`; on May 21 the user chose to commit them under `docs/research/`, and confirmed PR #932 merged. Old issue bodies still referencing the superpowers paths are stale. [S1]

Board acceptance is more than green CI: complete routing, correct/available BOM and footprints, peer review, physical rails ±5%, 24-hour thermal soak and functional tests. ATT-345 DoD requires the assembled stack actually working. Historical decisions to call skeletons or toolchain work “Done” do not establish physical acceptance. [S2–S5]

- “JLC sources the real EasyEDA footprint at fab” does not repair incorrect Gerber pads. The May 23–30 corrections establish that copper geometry had to be fixed in the design itself.
- “BOM PNs populated/verified” did not guarantee the right value, component class, pin count, package, body clearance or assembly method. Actual JLC uploads contradicted multiple early “ready” claims.

- The research and plan disagree on exact PHY pin numbering and strap placement: research says REFCLKO pin 14, plan’s map says pin 18; research strap table and plan code also differ. “Transcribed from datasheet” is not enough here. ## 6. Real blockers and unresolved research inputs

### 6.1 Electrical/topology blockers

1. **Power budget is inconsistent and the 1 A buck does not meet the recorded system loads.** ATT-378 proposes ≥2 A continuous and ~1.4 A load; PoE decision record selects MP9486A at 1 A and claims the whole stack ≤1 A. ATT-377 Q9 actually totals **2.760 A peak at +5 V**, requiring an LED duty≤25% firmware rule. Core POWER-BUDGET at `dedd98842` totals **2.230 A typical / 3.690 A peak**, while incorrectly asserting af PoE can provide **4 A at 5 V** (20 W before losses, inconsistent with 12.95 W PD input). No measured budget is present. Resolve LED/backlight limits, PHY/local losses, allowable peak duration and converter/connector ratings. [S9, S11, S12]
2. **Power-path isolation and pair-set acceptance need a new verified baseline.** Non-isolated topology and restricted jack path were approved, but the supporting compliance/isolation explanation and A/B naming are unreliable. Distinguish user approvals from actual standard requirements and current system exposure through USB/DC/other connectors. ATT-378’s “tie” between cable and SELV ground also cannot be treated as an approved isolated design. [S6, S9, S13]
3. **Manufacturer pin/strap validation remains open.** Inconsistent LAN8720A maps, WS3203 assumed compatibility, Class-0 resistor values, REGOFF/INTSEL behaviour, crystal package/loading and PHY address are historical assertions. Plan includes conflicting strap pull networks and is not a reliable schematic transcription. [S9, S10]
4. **HV/transient parts need verification**, including SMAJ58A actual clamp under chosen surge, capacitor voltage ratings and the **40 V SS34** used as catch diode in a claimed 37–57 V input buck. Catalog class checks cannot prove these margins. The shield capacitor is called Y2/2 kV in prose but modelled as a generic C0402 in the plan, another unresolved PN/package/rating contradiction. [S9, S10]

### 6.2 Layout, connector and evidence blockers

5. **Complete routing and clean meaningful DRC are not demonstrated.** May 23/28/30 comments explicitly leave missing PHY routes, QFN pad-to-pad zero-clearance and 37–75 µm trace-to-pad shortfalls. These are real pre-fab problems even if “pre-existing.” Historical PoE lint is `tscircuit-cli build index.tsx --ignore-warnings`; green status is not equivalent to a clean board. A config declaring jlcpcb4 does not by itself prove impedance or matching. [S6, S14]
6. **Final DFM alignment not confirmed.** Last saved PoE comment is an agent rotation fix, awaiting re-upload. Core’s May 30 remaining blockers require clear screenshots/orientation information for C6, USB-C and U_FLASH. D_VBUS collision was moved in `8e0e2222`, but C6 shift sign and flash/USB alignment remained uncertain. [S5, S6]
7. **RMII end-to-end timing remains uncoordinated.** PHY clock direction is settled. ATT-377/Core draft use ±5 mm groups and 33 Ω series near J_POE on Core; PoE doc uses 10 Ω RX-output resistors and flags REF_CLK_OUT timing. ATT-402 proposes ±5 mm group matching, ±0.5 mm “pair-skew,” REF_CLK reference, but has no discussion or agreed result. RMII is single-ended; define precisely what pair-skew means rather than borrowing differential-pair terms. Validate route across both boards/connector and source-side termination. [S5, S11, S15]
8. **Mechanical/mating and JLC assembly eligibility remain incomplete.** Actual board sizes diverge from shared docs; PoE/Core historical J_POE PNs differ. Signal freeze cannot prove header/socket mating, orientation, keying, current rating, mated height or THT service eligibility. [S7–S10]
9. **Physical pipeline proof was not recorded as finished.** ATT-348 is Done as of May 22, but its comments explicitly await boards, cost, lead time, SPL and smoke results. User exercised JLC upload/matching preview, not recorded receipt/functional proof. ATT-354 requires photos/video and real link/ping, NFC, WiFi, display/touch, beeper and diode-OR tests; its only comment is a sync notice. No physical PoE negotiation/MDIO/link/24 h result appears in the read context. [S3, S4, S16]
