# Mobile Usage (PWA)
<!-- llms-description: How to install Attraccess as a Progressive Web App on iOS and Android, with updates and offline support. -->

Attraccess is a Progressive Web App (PWA). This means you can install it on your phone or tablet and use it like a native app – no app store required.

## What is a PWA?

A Progressive Web App runs in your browser but can be installed on your home screen. Once installed, it:

- Opens in its own window (no browser address bar)
- Can be launched from your home screen like any other app
- Works offline for basic functionality
- Receives updates automatically

## Install on iOS (iPhone / iPad)

1. Open **Safari** and navigate to your Attraccess instance
2. Log in to your account
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up)
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
5. Give the app a name (or keep the default) and tap **Add**
6. Attraccess now appears on your home screen

> [!NOTE]
> On iOS, you must use Safari for installing PWAs. Other browsers (Chrome, Firefox) do not support this feature on iOS.

## Install on Android

1. Open **Chrome** and navigate to your Attraccess instance
2. Log in to your account
3. Tap the **three-dot menu** in the top right corner
4. Tap **Install app** (or "Add to Home screen")
5. Confirm by tapping **Install**
6. Attraccess now appears on your home screen and in your app drawer

> [!NOTE]
> On some Android devices, you may see a banner at the bottom of the page prompting you to install the app. Tap it to install directly.

## Using Attraccess on Mobile

Once installed, you can use Attraccess on your phone just like on a desktop:

- Browse available resources
- Start and end usage sessions
- View your usage history
- Manage your account

The interface adapts to smaller screens automatically.

## App Updates

When a new version of Attraccess is available, the app downloads it in the background while you keep working:

1. A short notice tells you an update is downloading.
2. A small circular progress indicator appears in the bottom-right corner. Drag it (or use the arrow keys while it is focused) to any other corner if it is in the way; Attraccess remembers your choice.
3. When the download is done, the indicator turns into an **Update ready** card. Choose **Reload now** to switch to the new version, or **Later** to shrink it to a small button you can tap again.

The update is also applied automatically when it is safe to do so: when you switch to another tab or app, after 5 minutes without any activity, or when you reload or close the page. Unsaved input on a page can be lost when this happens.

## Offline Support

Attraccess provides basic offline functionality:

- You can open the app and view previously loaded content
- Full functionality requires an internet connection to communicate with the server
