# Dashboard
<!-- llms-description: How to pin pages and resources to your personal dashboard, reorder them, and start sessions from it. -->

Your dashboard collects the pages and resources you use most in one place. Every user has their own dashboard, and it follows you to every browser and device you sign in on.

Open it with **Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar.

## Pin a page or a resource

1. Open the page or resource you want to keep at hand.
2. Select the **star** in its header (**Pin to dashboard**). The star fills in.

You can pin:

- every page in the sidebar that you are allowed to open, except the dashboard itself and links that leave Attraccess
- pages of plugins whose plugin offers them for the dashboard
- any resource, from its detail page

To unpin, select the filled star again, or the crossed-out star on the item's card on the dashboard.

## Your start page

As long as you have pinned nothing, Attraccess still opens on the resource overview. After your first pin, opening Attraccess shows your dashboard instead. Unpin everything to go back to the resource overview.

## Cards on the dashboard

- **Page cards** show the page's icon and name, plus a counter where the sidebar shows one (for example unread messages). Select the page name to open the page.
- **Resource cards** show the resource's status and its session controls. You can start and stop your own session right there, under the same rules as on the resource page: you need an introduction, supervised starts ask for a supervisor, and so on. A running session of someone else shows its timer. Select **Open** to go to the resource page.

The cards update live while sessions start and end.

## Reorder the cards

Drag a card by its handle (the dotted grip at its top left) to a new place. With a keyboard, focus the handle, press Space, move the card with the arrow keys and press Space again. The new order is saved immediately. If it cannot be saved, the dashboard returns to the saved order and tells you.

## Pins you can no longer open

When you lose access to a pinned page, for example because a permission was removed or a plugin is no longer available, its card says **No longer accessible**. Select **Remove from dashboard** to remove it. If you keep it and get access back, the normal card returns.

When a pinned resource is deleted, its card disappears from every dashboard.
