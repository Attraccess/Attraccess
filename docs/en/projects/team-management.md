# Team Management

Projects support team-based collaboration. You can invite members, manage roles, and coordinate who works on your project.

## Inviting Members

1. Open your project from the **Projects** page
2. Go to the **Team** tab
3. Click **Invite Member**
4. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
5. Click **Send Invitation**

<!-- TODO: Screenshot of the invite member dialog -->

The invited person will receive an email with a link to accept the invitation.

> [!NOTE]
> The invited user must have an Attraccess account. If they do not have one yet, they need to register first before they can accept the invitation.

## Accepting an Invitation

When you receive a project invitation:

1. Open the invitation email
2. Click the **Accept Invitation** link
3. You will be redirected to Attraccess and added to the project

> [!TIP]
> Check your spam folder if you do not see the invitation email. Ask your administrator to verify the [SMTP settings](setup/smtp-email.md) if emails are not being delivered.

## Managing Team Roles

Project owners can manage team member roles:

1. Open the project from the **Projects** page
2. Go to the **Team** tab
3. Click on a team member to view or change their role

## Removing Members

1. Open the project and go to the **Team** tab
2. Click the remove icon next to the member you want to remove
3. Confirm the removal

> [!NOTE]
> Removing a member from a project does not delete their usage history. Past sessions linked to the project remain in the records.
